B.S.E. Thesis

Thermal Behaviour of a Lithium Self-Field Magnetoplasmadynamic Thruster

Thesis details

Princeton University, August 2023–April 2024. Advised by Professor Edgar Y. Choueiri. Recognized through induction into Sigma Xi.

Numerical analysis and experimental validation of the thermal behaviour of a lithium self-field MPD thruster during its heat-up and plasma-firing phases, with recommendations for improving future measurements and models.
