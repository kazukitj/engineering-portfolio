Kazuki Tojo is an AeroAstro Ph.D. candidate in the MIT Gas Turbine Lab, examining the automation of flow mechanism extraction from CFD solutions, using AI and ML, under Professor Masha Folk. His interests lie in the thermofluids of aerospace propulsion. He has previously worked on compressor rotating stall under Ed Greitzer, electric propulsion (MPDT) under Edgar Choueiri, MEMS heat transfer gas sensing under Kam Chana & John Coull and drop coalescence under Howard Stone.
