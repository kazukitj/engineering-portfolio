Kazuki Tojo is an AeroAstro S.M.-Ph.D. student in the MIT Gas Turbine Lab, investigating compressor rotating stall under Professor Ed Greitzer. His interests lie in the thermofluids of aerospace propulsion. He was previously at Princeton University and the University of Oxford, where he worked on MEMS gas sensing through heat transfer, electric propulsion (MPDT), and drop coalescence.
